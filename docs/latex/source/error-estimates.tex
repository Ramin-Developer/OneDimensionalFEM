\chapter{Error Estimates} \label{cha:Error_Estimates}
Having presented the mathematical basis of FEM in \cref{cha:FEM_Approach}, the next natural step would be to derive error
estimates for the method. This is the subject of this chapter, where we derive an error estimate for FEM in terms of the meshing
size and the analytical solution. The error estimate is presented for a simplified version of \cref{eqn:Normalized_Problem} in
$L^2$-norm. However, the same approach may also be applied to more complicated problems and/or norms.
