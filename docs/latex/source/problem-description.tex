\section{Problem Description} \label{cha:Problem_Desc}
Consider the one-dimensional \gls{BVP} of modeling the deformation of a \gls{bar} under the axial load. The \gls{bar} has length
\gls{L} and a circular cross-sectional area \glssymbol{ABar}, where \gls{z} is the Cartesian axial coordinate. Left end
of the \gls{bar} is walled, while right end is subjected to the axial force \gls{PBar}. Furthermore, let
$\glssymbol{fBar} = \gls{fBar}$ denote the load at point $z$ in the \gls{bar}. We are interested in the \gls{bar}'s displacement
function $\glssymbol{uBar} = \gls{uBar}$, see \cref{fig:Problem_Description}.
