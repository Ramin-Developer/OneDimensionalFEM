\begin{abstract}

This report is meant as an introduction to \gls{FEM}. \Gls{FEM} is a numerical approach for solving ordinary and partial
\glspl{diffEq}. First we derive the governing equations for a mechanical one-dimensional \gls{BVP} and normalize it.
Before introduction of FEM-concepts we also study some variations of the problem with available analytical solutions. \\

Next we cover the mathematical bvackground of FEM including variational formulation, discretization, and the concept of truncated
solution. A whole chapter is also dedicated to deriving error estimates for a slightly simplified version of the problem. \\

Finite Element-solution is typically a truncated series of \glspl{basisFunction}. Any function satisfying the necessary
conditions may be chosen as \glspl{basisFunction}. However, because of their simplicity, polynomial functions with
\gls{localSupport} are a very popular choice, so we apply both linear and cubic polynomials. Since we also had the \gls{analSol}
we analyzed the error development and eventual \gls{solConv} for both linear and cubic \gls{basisFunction}.
\end{abstract}
